About SERVORA

Home services shouldn't require a leap of faith.

SERVORA was built on a simple idea: booking a plumber or an electrician should feel as safe and transparent as booking a cab. Every professional on our platform is identity-verified before they ever see a job request, every payment is held in escrow until the work is confirmed, and every booking is tracked in real time — for the customer's peace of mind, and the professional's protection.

Our principles

Services you can trust — literally.

Verified identity

Aadhaar, PAN and live selfie checks before any professional goes live on the platform.

Escrow payments

Money is held securely and only released once you confirm the job is complete.

Live tracking

Know exactly where your professional is, from acceptance to arrival.

Real ratings

Every review comes from a completed, paid booking — no fake listings.

Where we're headed

Mahendragarh first. Then the rest of Haryana. Then India.

We're launching block by block inside Mahendragarh district — Narnaul, Mahendragarh, Kanina, Ateli, Nangal Chaudhary, Nizampur, Sihma and Satnali — covering all 5 major towns and all 372 villages first, before expanding to neighbouring districts like Rewari and Bhiwani, then the rest of Haryana. The backend already supports multiple districts, cities, languages and currencies, so each expansion is a configuration change, not a rebuild.
